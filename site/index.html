Vencord, redistributed and improved. Extra plugins, a faster start, OpenAsar, and an app that installs exactly what you choose into any Discord.

What you get

Everything from Vencord, plus these. Each plugin and optional patch can be turned off, and stays off across updates.

Install

Two ways, same result. Both build Vencord from source with your selection and only swap it in once every step verifies.

Linux · one line

Shell installer

Needs git, node, pnpm and python3. Detects Stable, PTB, Canary and Development, native or Flatpak.

Linux · Windows

Vencord Forge

A desktop app: pick the Discord, plugins, patches and OpenAsar, then install. Needs nothing preinstalled; it fetches Node.js when missing.

Releases

How it works

No fork to drift, no prebuilt blobs: the newest Vencord, rebuilt with our changes on your machine.

  1. Fetch the newest upstream Vencord and this overlay.
  2. Copy in the custom plugins you kept and apply the patches you kept.
  3. Type-check against upstream. If upstream moved, fall back to the last version that built.
  4. Build, then verify every custom plugin is really in the bundle.
  5. Install the runtime, the loader and OpenAsar transactionally, verify the whole chain, and roll back on any failure.
  6. Update automatically later, from Discord's settings or on schedule, keeping your selection.
Not affiliated with Discord, Vencord or OpenAsar. Client modifications are against Discord's Terms of Service; use them at your own risk. Everything changed is backed up, and "Restore stock Discord" in Forge undoes it.
